Channel Analytics

A private analytics tool for YouTube channels owned by its operator.

Channel Analytics reads performance data for a small number of YouTube channels that belong to the person who runs it, so those channels' videos can be reviewed and compared.

It is not a public service. Only the owner of each channel authorizes access, and only to their own channels.

What it does

  • Reads channel and video statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, audience demographics, and revenue reports) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API.
  • Uses read-only access. It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything.
  • Keeps the results in private storage for the operator's own analysis.

See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is handled.
